Overview

Nemesis Protocol monitors MNQ futures during the 9:30–11:00 AM ET session window. It identifies the prior session’s high and low as reference liquidity pools, then waits for a manipulation sweep of one level before the distribution phase drives price in the opposite direction.

Entries require multi-timeframe confluence: a 4-hour, 1-hour, 30-minute, and 15-minute structural bias, a market structure break, and a fair value gap as the trigger zone. Every entry immediately places an OCA bracket (stop + limit target) anchored to the actual fill price — not the intended entry — to correctly account for slippage.

When conditions are ambiguous the bot exits at break-even: 57% of entries cost nothing. This selectivity is the primary driver of the 7.38× profit factor — losses are bounded and rare, winners run to structural targets.

# Fires on every partial + complete fill of the entry market order.
# Bracket is placed AFTER the fill so stop/target are anchored to
# the actual execution price — not the intended entry — accounting
# for slippage on each individual leg.

@ib.fillEvent
def on_fill(trade: Trade, fill: Fill):
    if trade.orderStatus.filled < trade.order.totalQuantity:
        return  # wait for the complete fill before placing bracket

    actual_fill = trade.orderStatus.avgFillPrice
    slippage    = actual_fill - state.entry_price

    if state.active_dir == "long":
        stop   = round(actual_fill - state.risk_pts,      2)
        target = round(actual_fill + state.target_offset, 2)
    else:
        stop   = round(actual_fill + state.risk_pts,      2)
        target = round(actual_fill - state.target_offset, 2)

    log.info(
        f"[FILL] {state.active_dir.upper()} x{state.contracts} @ {actual_fill:.2f}"
        f"  slippage {slippage:+.2f} pts | stop {stop:.2f} | target {target:.2f}"
    )

    # OCA group: when one order fills, the other is automatically cancelled
    place_oca_bracket(ib, contract, state, stop, target)
Engineering Features
01
Async event-driven architecture
Built on ib_insync’s asyncio event loop. Fill callbacks, order status updates, and portfolio events are handled asynchronously — the main loop never blocks waiting for IBKR responses.
02
OCA bracket order management
Every entry places a One-Cancels-All bracket (stop + limit target). Break-even management re-builds the OCA group with explicit cancellation of both legs to prevent orphaned resting orders.
03
SMT divergence detection
Compares real-time MES and MNQ 1-minute bars to detect Smart Money Technique divergences — when MES breaks a session extreme that MNQ hasn’t, the stop is automatically moved to break-even.
04
Dynamic position sizing
Contracts are calculated per-trade to risk approximately $300 regardless of stop distance. Max 8 contracts enforced. Sizing anchors to actual structural levels, not fixed point distances.
05
After-hours order handling
Force-flat at 4:00 pm ET via GTC + outsideRth market orders, designed to fill during the GLOBEX session open at 6:00 pm when the daily halt ends. Survives the 5–6 pm CME halt window.
06
Stuck-order recovery
If the bracket target stays in PendingSubmit for >2 minutes — indicating a silent IBKR rejection — the bot detects the stall and automatically re-places the full OCA bracket.
